### Highlight Projects & Technical Experience

**Industrial IoT — Web & Backend**
> **[SISMO — Silo Stock Monitoring & Web Dashboard](https://github.com/Pond141205/FRA261-262_SISMO):** Full-Stack & System Integration Developer. Built the **Python** backend managing inventory metrics, silo data structures, and multi-branch access, plus an interactive **web dashboard** with role-based security for real-time stock monitoring — replacing manual stock estimation with automated, cloud-connected tracking.

**Mechatronics & Control**
> **1-DOF Robotic Pick-and-Place Manipulator:** Lead Mechatronics & Control Developer. Designed and assembled an industrial electrical enclosure powered by **STM32 (NUCLEO-G474RE)**, integrating **24V NPN proximity sensors**, motor stall protection, and safety relay interlocks. Analyzed natural frequencies and payload swing dynamics for fast, deterministic cycle times.

**Estimation & Signal Processing**
> **[Dynamic Estimation & Motor Control Lab](https://github.com/Lengkung55/LAB4_PartLAB3):** Simulation & Control Engineer. Implemented **Kalman filtering** and automated spike removal in **MATLAB** to process raw sensor telemetry, tuned **PID** parameters in **Simulink** to minimize steady-state error and overshoot, and validated DC motor torque-speed curves against theoretical models.

**Mechanical & Industrial Design**
> **Power Transmission & Industrial Cabinet Design:** Sized transmission shafts, bearings, and gears via **Misumi** catalogs with critical speed analysis to prevent resonant failure. Modeled 3D industrial control boxes to **TAMCO CB-07** spec in **SolidWorks**, separating 24V/220V power with modular DIN-rail layout.

**Academic Software**
> **[FRA261](https://github.com/Lengkung55/FRA261)** — full-stack web system, backend logic, and database design. **[FRA142-143](https://github.com/Lengkung55/FRA142-143)** — foundational algorithms and programming workflows.
