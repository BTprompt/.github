### 👤 About

Undergraduate Robotics Engineering student at **KMUTT's Institute of Field Robotics (FIBO)**, 3rd year. Works across the full stack of a robot — power electronics, motor control, sensor integration, and application software.

**⚡ Highlights**
- 🍽️ **MOD EAT** — full-stack food-ordering web app (React · TypeScript · Bun/Elysia · MySQL) with QR-code payment & slip verification
- 🏓 **Squash Ball Hitting Robot** — force/momentum/bounce-coefficient calculation + custom AC-DC power supply build
- 🦾 **1-DOF Pick-and-Place Robot** — ≤1% overshoot tolerance, PLC-standard control box design
- ⚙️ Lab work in motor system identification, Hall-effect sensing, BLDC trapezoidal commutation
