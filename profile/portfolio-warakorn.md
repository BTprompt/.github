### Highlight Projects & Technical Experience

**Full-Stack Web Development**
> **MOD EAT:** Built an online food-ordering platform for a university food court, solving queue congestion and disorganized ordering. Designed a QR-code payment flow with automated slip verification before confirming an order to the vendor.

**Robotics — Dynamics & Control**
> **Squash Ball Hitting Robot:** Designed a robot to intercept a free-falling squash ball and return it to a target zone, calculating required force, momentum, and the ball's bounce coefficient. Built the power supply stage, converting AC line voltage to regulated DC.

**Pick-and-Place Automation**
> **1-DOF Pick-and-Place Robot:** Built a robot that picks a ROD part from one of 4 randomized positions and places it at a fixed target, achieving ≤1% overshoot tolerance. Designed the control box's power distribution to industrial (PLC-style) standards.

**Embedded Systems Labs**
> **Motor Characterization & Sensing:** Ran System Identification labs to extract motor characteristics. Implemented Hall-effect sensor readout via NUCLEO-G474RE. Analyzed trapezoidal commutation of a DC brushless motor using an oscilloscope.
