### 🚀 Highlight Projects & Technical Experience

**🍽️ Full-Stack Web Development**
> **MOD EAT:** Built an online food-ordering platform for a university food court, solving queue congestion and disorganized ordering. Designed a QR-code payment flow with automated slip verification (checks destination account, timestamp, and amount) before confirming an order to the vendor.

**🏓 Robotics — Dynamics & Control**
> **Squash Ball Hitting Robot:** Designed a robot to intercept a free-falling squash ball and return it to a target zone, calculating required force, momentum, and the ball's bounce (restitution) coefficient. Built the power supply stage, converting AC line voltage to regulated DC to drive the actuation system.

**🦾 Pick-and-Place Automation**
> **1-DOF Pick-and-Place Robot:** Built a robot that picks a ROD part from one of 4 randomized positions and places it at a fixed target, achieving ≤1% overshoot tolerance on travel distance. Designed the control box's power distribution, following industrial (PLC-style) electrical control standards, and integrated the end effector with the client system's base controller.

**⚡ Embedded Systems Labs**
> **Motor Characterization & Sensing:** Ran System Identification labs to extract motor characteristics across varying input signal levels and voltages. Implemented Hall-effect sensor readout via NUCLEO-G474RE and studied magnetic-field behavior. Analyzed trapezoidal commutation behavior of a DC brushless motor using an oscilloscope.
